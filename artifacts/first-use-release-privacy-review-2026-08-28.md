# Privacy review: First-use account replacement candidate

## Risk decision
CLEAR FOR REVIEW

## Data-flow summary
First-use input (optional display name, interface language, Bible edition, and one public starter-quest slug) → transient React state → the existing device-local QuestOS persistence boundary. A guest journey remains on that device until the reader exports or clears it. In the account-release profile, an anonymous, no-credential, no-store RPC checks the reviewed Supabase account-availability contract before an auth client starts; a reader who explicitly creates or enters an account may then send an email address or Apple credential to Supabase/Apple, store the resulting native session in Keychain, and sync eligible journey records to per-user Supabase rows. The account-release profile pins Plausible analytics and native commerce off. Other builds keep analytics opt-in, DNT/GPC-aware, and restricted to the non-textual allowlist; no prayer, reflection, journal, note, identity, token, private URL, or starter-quest text is added by this change. Settings retains local export/clear, synced-data deletion, account deletion, and the documented provider-retention boundaries.

## Findings

## Verification required
- Re-run lint, TypeScript, the full unit suite, eleven optimized-browser journeys, headers/service-worker/observability checks, production audit, guarded account-release export, Release iPhone compilation, and exact-artifact verification after this review commit.
- On the signed exact candidate, prove remote account availability off/on behavior, email and Apple auth, restore, offline/reconnect, sign-out, clear, deletion, reinstall, old-client containment, and cross-account isolation with two synthetic accounts on two iPhones.
- Complete VoiceOver, Full Keyboard Access, large Dynamic Type, reduced motion, contrast, safe-area, and keyboard checks on physical hardware.
- Have the privacy/legal owner approve App Privacy answers, account-data disclosure, live entity/support identity, retention/deletion language, visual rights, and the exact in-app policy; record a name and UTC timestamp.
- Confirm the deployed source and advertised schema match the merged candidate before allowing customer traffic.

## Residual risk and owner
This review establishes a minimized and fail-closed source design, not production safety. The privacy/legal owner owns policy, disclosure, and provider-retention approval; the account/QA owner owns signed two-user isolation and deletion evidence; and the release owner must keep account availability off or roll back if the exact-binary, schema, provider, or live-origin contracts differ.
