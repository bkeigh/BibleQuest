# Content review: First-use starter quest pool

## Decision
APPROVE FOR HUMAN REVIEW

## What works
- The four proposed items already belong to the reviewed free catalogue and retain their existing copy, metadata, and Scripture references.
- Every item is solo, gentle, five or ten minutes, and free of sensitivity tags, contact requests, spending, reconciliation, food restriction, or subscription pressure.
- Silence, Scripture, reflection, and gratitude give first-time readers a meaningful range without presenting an advanced formation task as a small first step.

## Issues and evidence
- [minor] This is a new high-visibility placement even though the content itself is unchanged. The content owner must confirm that all four invitations are suitable for an unknown first-time reader before the candidate is frozen.
- [minor] “Three Small Thanks” says “Before the day ends.” It remains doable at any time, but the owner should confirm that this time framing is acceptable in a pool that can surface in the morning.

## Scripture support
Verified against the checked-in public-domain WEB corpus and the existing reviewed seed records. Psalm 46:10 supports making room for stillness; Psalm 23:1–6 supports slow, restful Scripture reading; James 1:17 supports noticing received goodness; and 1 Thessalonians 5:18 supports a brief gratitude practice. None of the references is being repurposed to support contact, financial action, or a claim of guaranteed spiritual outcome.

## Suggested revision
No seed-copy change is proposed. Approve or remove individual slugs from the onboarding allowlist without rewriting the reviewed catalogue records.
